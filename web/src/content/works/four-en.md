A class T-shirt design for Class 4 of Network and New Media. The gallery includes design mockups and a detail photograph of the physical T-shirt.

The graphics combine the number 4, arcs and brush-like strokes in navy and silver. The front emblem is **6 cm** in diameter; the back graphic is **20 cm** wide.
